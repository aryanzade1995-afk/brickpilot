# Formstead house-idea images

Architectural images rendered in Blender from Formstead's procedural house geometry. These are design examples, not images of the current user's exact building or selected products.

Some images include the Ferrari 458 Italia model by vicent091036, adapted under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Adaptations include scale, placement and materials within the procedural scene.
