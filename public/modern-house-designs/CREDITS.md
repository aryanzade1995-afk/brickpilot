# Modern Box reference renders

All four WebP thumbnails are cropped three-quarter Blender renders of the same Formstead-generated two-storey house. They use plan `plan-460b9ad3e7d66c21`, architectural seed 41 and façade seed 77085. The filename identifies the geometry family. The render script and Blender materials ship with this repository.

The parked Ferrari 458 Italia uses geometry by vicent091036 under CC BY 4.0, with Formstead materials and placement. The thumbnails crop and compress the rendered scene.
