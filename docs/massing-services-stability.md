# Massing services and exterior identity

Pipeline controls are a separate layer. Inspection changes pipe depth testing;
it no longer ghosts or recolours the accepted architecture. Source-aligned
horizontal water runs are embedded on both wall centre planes at corners,
with pipe diameter plus a 20 mm concept envelope each side. Floor feeders
retain their exact junction endpoints. Column/beam/slab conflicts do not
authorize new holes. Fixture traps remain accessible; suitable waste drops
meet a sufficiently thick recorded wall before the original 2D floor branch.
Oversized pipes still require a duct or a reviewed alternate route. Existing
exterior bypass proposals are explicitly retained as review items, not
misrepresented as concealed or approved connections.

Layout records now preserve the exterior namespace and accepted seed across
edits, save/reload and undo. The real geometry ID still changes for actual
plan edits; the exterior random recipe, family and palette keep their source
identity. A changed wall/footprint must change the corresponding geometry;
the previous GLB is never relabelled as an edited house. Legacy layout
records remain readable and capture their source identity when edited.

Optional shade skins use the CC0 Kenney City Kit Commercial 2.1:
https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial
https://opengameart.org/content/city-kit-commercial

Downloaded from the author's OpenGameArt mirror on 2026-10-28. Only canopy
skins are retained; existing accepted supports, guards and balcony slabs
remain unchanged. The variations cover only explicitly tagged balconies
that already have an overhead frame. No arbitrary balcony/window is invented.
Sage retains the pack's material; sand and terracotta are visual tint variants.
These concept add-ons do not establish a product price or structural adequacy.

`visual-model-balcony-shade` saves through the existing brief specifications,
excluded from geometry and architectural finish signatures. Original GLB and
images retain their original contents; the live combined GLB includes the
selected overlay. Reopening strips tagged baked shade overlays before the
current one is applied, preventing duplicates. The original option removes
the add-on without rebuilding the accepted model.
