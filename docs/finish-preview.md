# Finishes & Cost preview

The existing specification panel remains the entry point. Selecting an option changes its preview immediately; **Use this** persists it through the existing plan-linked cost engine.

`optionImage.ts` resolves one source for cards, details, countertop cards and 3D surfaces. Manufacturer swatches are copied byte-for-byte into `public/specs/products`; `imageAssets.json` records original URLs and SHA-256 hashes. Provenance and manufacturer permission flags remain in the catalogues.

Photographed surface swatches use sRGB, white unlit materials with tone mapping disabled, and normalised box-face UVs. Scene lights cannot wash them out. Installation/product photographs are displayed intact alongside the geometry; they are not falsely tiled as seamless textures. Glass remains transparent, and the legacy glazing options explicitly label their metal sample as a frame reference. Clear/toughened glass and price-only allowance tiers can share an appearance.

The renderer keeps its Canvas and scene mounted across selections, refreshes meshes and textures, disposes replaced textures, and ignores late image-load callbacks. It draws continuously while the panel is open. Error boundaries reset on a different option; image failures show an explicit retry message. Other preview sections retain their lighting and texture repetition.

Only photographed options are offered for flooring, wall tiles/cladding, kitchen, doors, windows/glass/grills, railings/gates, waterproofing, painting, plumbing/sanitary and electrical. Their comparisons, filter facets, counts and detail sheets use the same list. Specifications with no photographed choices are omitted. Internal legacy IDs and rates are preserved so existing saved projects keep their original estimate until explicitly changed. Service allowances that merely carry concrete/metal/plaster proxy swatches are excluded from the photographed chooser too.

Added six Fenesta door configurations, six Q-railing configurations, two Orientbell wall swatches, two wood cabinet surfaces and an Onyx tile countertop. Existing opening dimensions and building geometry are untouched. Prices are provisional installed allowances, not manufacturer quotes.

Validation: production build; 37 focused tests covering data provenance, price isolation, quantities, geometry, per-room scope and saved selections; browser checks of 19 configurations across all requested sections. Ten paired screenshots showed changed pixels inside the 3D canvas, including clear versus double glazing. Applying a railing option and reopening the panel retained the selected ID; the test change was undone.

Sources are stored per product. Primary references include https://www.fenesta.com/door, https://q-railing.com/en-us/railings/systemfinder/, and https://www.orientbell.com/pgvt-onyx-pearl.

## Electrical, bathroom, waterproofing and paint update

`service-products.json` adds 73 official supplier catalogue images, unmodified local copies with hashes in `service-image-audit.json`. Six electrical categories have five choices each: ceiling fans, ceiling/downlights, bulbs, battens, switches/sockets and exhaust fans. Bathroom choices include six toilets (Indian squatting pan and Western wall-hung/floor-standing types), plus five each of basins, basin faucets, showerheads, tubs, enclosures and water heaters. Paint offers three interior products and a photographed exterior waterproof coating. Waterproofing offers one roof product and two wet-area systems; unpictured below-ground and specialist systems disappear from the chooser.

`servicePreviewGeometry.ts` builds component-specific shapes. Hollow ceramic shells use 96 segments, outer and inner profiles, and rounded rectangular/oval sections. Glass cabins include door hardware, sliding tracks or hinges; showers include nozzles and hand-shower context. Appliances vary in blade count, geometry, module layout, housing and size. These are installation illustrations, not manufacturer CAD or certified dimensions. Actual supplier images remain intact beside every model; supplier photography methods are not verified.

Waterproofing has an exploded substrate/preparation/coating/protection view with wall upturn, junction band and drain context. Display colours and thicknesses explain the system rather than prescribing application. Follow the official technical sheets. Paint uses an unlit exact digital shade and updates on input as well as change; changing product quality retains the shade. Different paint brands can share the same visible wall colour.

Seven additional component preferences use the existing scope and save/undo mechanism. Bathroom preferences inherit real measured bathroom locations, persist separately per room, and appear in specification schedules. Basins/showerheads stay within existing sanitary/fittings package allowances; optional tubs, cabins and extra lighting/ventilation require measured supplier quotes. They add no duplicate allowance or invented quantity. Existing fans, ceiling fittings, switches and heaters reprice their existing measured lines; sanitary/faucet choices clearly describe a whole-bathroom package allowance, not a quote for the pictured product alone.

Final validation: build and lint pass, 42 focused tests pass (including asset hashes, component models, room isolation, reports, saved selections, price isolation and unchanged geometry). Browser verification selected all 73 photographed service choices and confirmed ready canvases, the selected option ID, matching card/reference image and loaded source image. 33 canvas screenshots were nonblank; first/last pairs change actual pixels for every new component family except paint brands deliberately sharing the same chosen colour. Changed faucet/heater/batten/exhaust models were rechecked after visual fixes. Custom paint colour retains its value across product changes; saved fan and one-bathroom basin choices reopen correctly. Mobile fan and Indian-toilet previews work without horizontal overflow. Test selections were undone; no browser errors were recorded.
